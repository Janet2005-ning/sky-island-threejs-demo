# Recording and AE Checklist

## Browser Recording Prep

- Use 16:9 viewport.
- Close unrelated tabs and apps.
- Hide debug overlays.
- Confirm frame rate feels smooth.
- Confirm all click targets work.
- Do one full rehearsal before recording.

## Takes to Record

- Full flow from page load to completion.
- Close-up of camera fly-in.
- Close-up of block hover and click feedback.
- Completion reward moment.
- Optional slow mouse-look shot across the island map.

## AE / Editing Additions

- Soft hover sounds.
- Click/grab sound.
- Success chime.
- Subtle camera shake only on impact moments.
- Cyan glow around interactive UI.
- Gold glow for success.
- Short explanatory captions if needed.

## Final Export

- 1920x1080 or 2560x1440.
- 24 or 30 fps is enough for portfolio.
- Keep a clean version without subtitles if possible.
